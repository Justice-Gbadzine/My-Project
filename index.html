Company Background

Connecting Ghana & Beyond To Global Freight Networks

PERCULIAR LINES LIMITED (PLL) is a Ghanaian registered company, established in Accra in 2021 with specialty in Logistics and Removals as an International Freight Forwarder and Logistics Services Provider.

It is a Ghanaian resident company and projected to connecting to affiliates and consultants with operational working experience worldwide.

Our comprehensive logistics capabilities span Air Freight, Ocean Freight (FCL/LCL), Overland Haulage, Customs Brokerage, Global Pickup & Shipping, and professional Packing & Moving for individuals, businesses, and expatriates.

Our Logistics & Freight Services

Home Services
Core Services

End-to-End Supply Chain Solutions

Tailored logistics and removal services for commercial importers, exporters, businesses, and individual clients.

Air Freight

The precision-timed arrival of goods at your premises or on your customer's premises is what our air freight department aims at. Our customized air freight products allow you to coordinate your cargo even more flexibly and efficiently. Highly advanced information and communication technology helps us promptly react to eliminate administrative delays and track cargo in transit. Comprehensive coverage across major trade lanes to/from India to Asia Pacific, Latin America, Americas, Europe, Africa, Middle East, and Australia.

  • Customized Air Freight Solutions
  • Prompt Reaction to Eliminate Delays
  • Trade Lanes Across 7 Continents

Ocean Freight

Sea Transport is a vital part of the world economy. We offer the right system for all cargoes depending on nature, volume, and type by working out the best transportation arrangements and selecting appropriate carriers. Flexible service options include Full Container Load (FCL) and Less Than Container Load (LCL), container consolidation/deconsolidation, standard documentation, complex banking arrangements, and marine insurance coverage.

  • Full Container Load (FCL) & LCL Service
  • LCL Consolidation & Deconsolidation
  • Banking Arrangements & Marine Insurance

Haulage

We recognize the need of the hour. Therefore, we offer customer-specific transportation and ready solutions for part load or full truck load transportation. The availability of the right equipment, at the right place and at the right time, defines our uniqueness, resulting in shorter lead-times and a more reliable flow of goods.

  • Part Load & Full Truck Load (FTL)
  • Shorter Lead-Times & Reliable Flow
  • Specialized Fleet Equipment

Customs Brokerage

Customs clearance forms a major part in the logistics industry. Our customs clearance agents are experts in local specifications, conditions, and regulations. On-the-ground knowledge allows them to handle administrative tasks swiftly and without complications, advising on liabilities and government policy compliances.

  • Expert Local Regulatory Knowledge
  • Swift Complication-Free Clearance
  • Policy Compliance & Liability Advisory

Pickup and Shipping

With a deep understanding of the market, we maintain our focus on systemization and standardization so that we can arrange pickup delivery services effortlessly. Connected to a network of Freight Forwarding partner agencies globally where shipments are arranged for pickup and shipped on behalf of clients with the highest standards of professionalism.

  • Worldwide Partner Agency Network
  • Standardized Effortless Pickup & Dispatch
  • Highest Standards of Professionalism

Packing and Moving

We render services to expatriates, individuals, and businesses moving household goods and office assets from one place to another. All-inclusive relocation services including professional packing, stuffing and unstuffing, arranging, and moving items to be completely safe for shipping.

  • Expatriate & Household Goods Moving
  • Professional Packing, Stuffing & Unstuffing
  • All-Inclusive Safe Relocations
